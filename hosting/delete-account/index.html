Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your Parivaar account and the data associated with it.

Request account deletion

To delete your Parivaar account, send an email from the address you signed up with to:

pariverse94@gmail.com

  1. Use the subject line “Parivaar Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your Parivaar account (or mention that address in the email).
  3. We will verify the request and permanently delete your account within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when it is done.
If you signed in with Google, your account email is your Google account address.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (email & sign-in credentials)Permanently deleted
Profile (name, family details)Permanently deleted
Chores, meal plans, notes and other in-app dataPermanently deleted
Community posts you createdPermanently deleted
Retention: deletion is completed within 30 days of a verified request. No personal data is retained after deletion, except where a longer retention period is required by law.
