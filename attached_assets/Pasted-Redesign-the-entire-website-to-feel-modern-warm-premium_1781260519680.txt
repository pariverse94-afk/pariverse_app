Redesign the entire website to feel modern, warm, premium, and emotionally engaging for urban Indian mothers and nuclear families.

## Landing Page Redesign

### Hero Section (Above the Fold)

Remove the current hero section completely.

Replace it with a large animated scene occupying most of the screen height.

Create 4 animated family scenes that automatically transition every 3-4 seconds with smooth fade animations:

1. Mother organizing household chores while managing daily tasks.
2. Parents and children collaborating on household chores together.
3. Family planning weekly household activities and responsibilities.
4. Happy family enjoying quality time because household work is organized.

The animation style should be soft, friendly, modern, slightly illustrated, and emotionally warm. Avoid corporate or stock-photo aesthetics.

Overlay a simple headline:

**"Making Family Life Easier, Together."**

Subheadline:

**"Organize chores, coordinate responsibilities, and build happier homes."**

Below the headline add two prominent CTA buttons:

Primary Button:
**Join Waitlist**

Secondary Button:
**Get it on Play Store**

Buttons should remain visible on all screen sizes.
